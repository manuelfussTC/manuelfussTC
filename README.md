# Manuel Fuß

I work on AI-first software development and the practical use of LLMs in real engineering workflows. Most of my work focuses on agentic development, control loops and clean, predictable processes around LLMs. 

Pulse, my framework for controlled agentic development, grew out of real projects where teams needed reliability instead of endless demos. I use it to build prototypes, internal tools and full workflows that help companies ship faster without losing control over their code.

I share parts of this work in my repositories and keep refining the methods I use with teams every day.

## Current focus
- Agentic development with LLMs  
- Safe control loops and guardrails  
- AI-assisted software engineering  
- Practical integration of AI in enterprise environments  
- Building and refining the Pulse Framework  

## Repositories
The work here is a mix of experiments, prototypes and parts of the Pulse ecosystem as it grows.
